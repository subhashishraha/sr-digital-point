1. Who we are

SR Digital Point (“we”, “us”) is a local digital service centre in Janaphul, Habra, West Bengal. We help customers with online form fill-up, Aadhaar, Voter card, Ration card, Scholarship and Land & property related online services, printing & Xerox, photos, train and flight ticket booking, and design services (flex/banner, business card, logo and website design).

This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website, our app, WhatsApp, phone and at our shop, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It is written in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

2. Information we collect

a) When you send an enquiry on our website

Your name, mobile number, email address (optional), the service you need, your preferred contact method, preferred visit date (optional) and your message. You also confirm consent with a checkbox before sending.

b) When you contact us by WhatsApp or phone

Your phone number, WhatsApp name and anything you choose to send us (messages, photos or documents).

c) When we provide a service for you

Depending on the service you ask for, this may include:

  • Identity & contact details — name, date of birth, gender, address, mobile number, email.
  • ID numbers and copies of documents — e.g. Aadhaar, Voter ID (EPIC), Ration card, PAN, certificates.
  • Photographs and signatures (for photo services and application forms).
  • Education, income or family details needed for scholarship and other application forms.
  • Land and property details (e.g. plot / khatian / ROR information).
  • Travel details for ticket booking — passenger names, ages, ID details, journey dates.
  • Bank details only where an official form requires them (e.g. scholarship payouts).
  • Files, text, logos and photos you give us for printing or design work.

d) Information collected automatically

Our website does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not track you. The services that run parts of the website (listed in section 6) may log technical data such as your IP address and browser type, as any website server does.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry by call, WhatsApp or email, as you prefer.
  • To fill in and submit the applications, forms and bookings you ask us to do.
  • To print, scan, photograph or design what you ordered.
  • To tell you about the status of your work or ask for missing details.
  • To keep simple records of work done and to meet legal requirements.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Documents, OTPs & ID details

  • Original documents are checked and returned to you immediately.
  • Scans and copies are used only for your requested service and are deleted (or physically shredded) once the work is complete and no longer needed.
  • OTPs are requested only when a government or booking portal sends one to you for the service you asked for — and only while you are with us or on a call you started.
  • We never ask for your ATM PIN, UPI PIN, net-banking password or card CVV. If anyone claiming to be from SR Digital Point asks for these, do not share them.
  • Aadhaar details are used only for the purpose you consented to, and we do not keep copies of your Aadhaar after the work is done.

5–6. Sharing & third-party services

We share your information only when it is needed to do what you asked, or when the law requires it:

  • Government & official portals (for example UIDAI, Election Commission, Food & Supplies, scholarship and land-record portals) — when we submit an application for you. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Booking platforms (for example IRCTC and airlines) — when we book tickets for you.
  • Web3Forms — delivers the website enquiry form to our email inbox.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — if you choose to contact us on WhatsApp.
  • Google Maps — shows our location on the website; Google may collect data when you use the map.
  • Google Fonts, jsDelivr and GitHub Pages — supply the website’s fonts, icons and hosting.
  • Authorities — if required by law, court order or to prevent fraud.

7. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected:

  • Enquiry messages and emails — up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Document scans and photos — deleted once your work is complete.
  • Basic work records (name, service, date) — as long as required by law or for accounts.

8. How we protect your information

Work computers are password-protected, documents are kept out of public view during work, and copies are removed when no longer needed. Our website is served over a secure HTTPS connection. No method of storage or transfer is 100% secure, but we take reasonable care to protect your information.

9. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you and how it is used.
  • Ask us to correct or update inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to delete your information when it is no longer needed.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time (this will not affect work already completed).
  • Nominate a person to exercise these rights on your behalf.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 14.

10. Children

Services for anyone under 18 (for example scholarship forms or a child’s Aadhaar) are provided only with the involvement and consent of a parent or legal guardian.

11. Website & app storage on your device

Our website can be installed as an app. To load faster and work offline, it saves copies of its own pages, styles and images on your device. This does not include any personal information. You can remove it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing site data in your browser settings.

12. Important note

SR Digital Point is an independent service centre and is not a government office. Approval of any application is decided only by the concerned authority. Official government fees are charged as per the authority; our service charge is separate and will be told to you before the work begins.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with the “Last updated” date shown at the top.
